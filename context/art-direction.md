ASPHRA — ART DIRECTION
1. Visual Identity

Asphra uses a low-poly, semi-realistic visual style.

The game should combine the simplicity and accessibility of browser racing games with the recognizable appearance of real Formula racing circuits.

2. Visual Inspirations
PolyTrack: Low-poly geometry and simple visual presentation.
Slowroads.io: Environmental atmosphere and scenery.
Apex Formula Racing: Formula-style vehicles and racing presentation.
Real-world Formula 1 circuits: Trackside infrastructure and recognizable locations.
3. Visual Philosophy

Graphics do not need to be photorealistic.

However, the experience should feel convincing.

Players should be able to recognize circuits through:

Track layouts.
Corner shapes.
Grandstands.
Buildings.
Surrounding terrain.
Trees and vegetation.
Trackside structures.

The low-poly aesthetic should be intentional rather than unfinished-looking.

4. Vehicle Appearance

The player vehicle should resemble a Formula racing car.

Maintain:

Formula-style proportions.
Distinctive front and rear wings.
Open-wheel design.
Appropriate racing tires.
Consistent materials.

Avoid unnecessary visual complexity that negatively affects performance.

5. Track Appearance

The circuit should be the visual centerpiece.

Important elements:

Asphalt surface.
Track boundaries.
Kerbs.
Runoff areas.
Track markings.
Barriers.
Pit infrastructure.

Track details should reflect the actual circuit.

6. Environment

Environments should be recognizable recreations of real-world locations.

Examples of future details:

Monza's surrounding forest.
Circuit-specific grandstands.
Pit buildings.
Hills and mountains.
Trackside advertisements.
Fences and barriers.

These should use simplified geometry where possible.

7. Lighting and Atmosphere

The current scene includes a blue sky.

Future improvements may include:

Directional sunlight.
Ambient lighting.
Shadows.
Fog.
Time-of-day variations.
Environmental atmosphere.

Avoid expensive visual effects unless their performance cost is justified.

8. Camera Presentation

The initial camera should provide a clear view from above and behind the driver.

Future camera options may take inspiration from F1 25.

9. Performance

Visual quality must be balanced against browser performance.

Prefer:

Reusable geometry.
Instanced repeated objects.
Efficient materials.
Limited draw calls.
Appropriate level of detail.
Efficient shadow rendering.

Do not add expensive graphical effects simply for visual novelty.

10. Consistency

All new visual assets should follow the established low-poly, semi-realistic identity.

New circuits should look like part of the same game.
