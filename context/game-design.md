ASPHRA — GAME DESIGN
1. Game Concept

Asphra is a browser-based Formula driving game focused on free driving around faithfully recreated real-world Formula 1 circuits.

The game combines accessible low-poly visuals with a driving experience that aims to feel authentic and engaging.

The player should be able to select a circuit and drive around it freely.

2. Core Gameplay

The primary gameplay experience is free driving.

Players should be able to:

Select a circuit.
Spawn onto the selected circuit.
Drive around the entire circuit.
Accelerate, brake and steer.
Explore the circuit.
Experience the handling and characteristics of the vehicle.

Traditional race modes are not the immediate development priority.

3. Vehicle Handling

Handling should take inspiration from:

PolyTrack.
Slowroads.io.
Apex Formula Racing.

The intended experience should be accessible and enjoyable while maintaining convincing Formula-style vehicle behavior.

Important handling characteristics:

Acceleration.
Braking.
Steering.
Cornering.
Grip.
Speed-dependent handling.
Gear changes.
Vehicle stability.

The project should gradually improve realism without making driving unnecessarily difficult.

Exact physics behavior must be determined by the existing vehicle implementation and subsequent testing.

4. Vehicle Control

Player control is the primary driving mode.

The project includes:

Vehicle controls.
Vehicle physics.
Gearbox.
Vehicle limits.
Optional autodrive.

Autodrive is primarily a supplementary feature for experimentation, demonstrations and testing.

It should not replace player-controlled driving.

5. Autodrive

Autodrive is a fun optional feature.

It uses existing raceline-related functionality.

Future development may improve:

Cornering behavior.
Speed selection.
Braking.
Steering smoothness.
Raceline following.

Do not redesign autodrive without examining the existing implementation.

6. Camera System
Current Direction

The initial camera should follow the vehicle from above and behind the driver's head.

Future Direction

Camera options should eventually take inspiration from F1 25.

Possible future perspectives:

Standard chase camera.
Closer follow camera.
Higher chase camera.
Cockpit-style camera.
Additional configurable views.

These are planned possibilities, not current implemented features.

7. Future Gameplay

Potential future additions:

AI opponents.
Racing events.
Time trials.
Multiplayer.
Leaderboards.
Lap timing.

These features are not immediate priorities.

8. Track Selection

Players should eventually be able to select from a collection of real-world F1 circuits.

Each circuit should load its corresponding track data and environment.

The track selection menu should be modular so new circuits can be added without rewriting the game.

9. Gameplay Philosophy

Asphra should feel like a lightweight Formula driving experience rather than a conventional arcade racing game.

Driving feel and recognizable circuits are more important than photorealistic graphics.

The game should remain accessible to players using ordinary browser-compatible hardware.
