8. Rules
Only record reproducible or reasonably evidenced issues.
Distinguish bugs from missing features.
Include reproduction steps whenever possible.
Do not silently remove old bug reports.
Update status when an issue is resolved.
Verify fixes before marking bugs as resolved.
